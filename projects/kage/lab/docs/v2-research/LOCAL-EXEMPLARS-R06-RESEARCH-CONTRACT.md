# Local Exemplars R06 — Research Contract

## Objective

Turn the strongest local HTML experiments in `Downloads` into evidence-backed V2 design knowledge. The purpose is not to copy pages or import one-off assets; it is to identify reusable visual, interaction, motion, and delivery principles that can constrain later model generation.

## Evidence boundary

- Primary evidence: local HTML source, local browser runtime, screenshots, responsive states, and console/network behavior.
- E4: behavior directly observed in a running browser.
- E3: implementation confirmed from source but not fully observed at runtime.
- E2: a plausible inference from visual or source evidence.
- E1: an unverified hypothesis; it must not be promoted into the capability catalog.

## Required coverage

| Surface | Required checks | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three user-selected pages | Source, first view, scroll/interaction, responsive, runtime health | Three capability maps |
| Same-batch HTML experiments | Static inventory and targeted runtime checks | Promote / hold / exclude decision |
| Older 3D and product experiments | Technique and product-value classification | Technical-lab or presentation-case label |
| Cross-case synthesis | Repeated strengths, gaps, combinations, and constraints | Bounded reusable principles |
| V2 project | Typed research data, research note, and verification | Durable project knowledge |

## Experience architecture

This research follows an editorial evidence flow:

1. Inventory candidates.
2. Observe the strongest representative states.
3. Separate visual result from implementation mechanism.
4. Separate reusable principle from one-off asset dependence.
5. Promote only principles supported by sufficient evidence.

## Promotion rules

A case may be promoted only when it has a coherent visual result, a legible interaction or narrative role, and enough source/runtime evidence to explain why it works. Broken pages, generic duplicates, and pages whose key effect depends on unavailable media remain in the research record but are not promoted as exemplars.

## Non-goals

- Do not copy local HTML files or their external media into the Kage project.
- Do not treat every attractive screenshot as a reusable capability.
- Do not add new generator constraints before the cross-case synthesis is complete.
- Do not update generic skills from this single local batch.

## Completion criteria

- The selected pages and relevant neighboring files are inventoried and classified.
- Every promoted case has strengths, limitations, implementation mechanism, and evidence level.
- Repeated principles are expressed as composable constraints rather than fixed templates.
- The result is represented in V2 project data and documentation and passes existing validation.
