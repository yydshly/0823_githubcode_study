- Built-in image generation produced a wide nocturnal biomaterial greenhouse environment.
- Three transparent-cutout attempts were rejected because file inspection reported `Format24bppRgb` and no real alpha channel.
- The accepted route is an honest full-bleed environment anchor, not a fake transparent cutout or central poster.
