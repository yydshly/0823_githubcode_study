# Case Curation Correction R25

## Design contract

- **Entry mode:** revision-led correction.
- **Request revision:** R25 — replace mechanically selected generated examples with the user's intended evidence hierarchy.
- **Target user and context:** a researcher evaluating whether a natural-language idea can become a polished Three.js webpage.
- **Desired first impression:** one complete model-refined work leads; two workbench capability examples explain the technical breadth.
- **Visual ambition:** immersive.
- **Experience architecture:** hybrid editorial overview linking to full-screen spatial stages.
- **Visual constraints:** the final work must be shown at its strongest full-screen route; the overview must not compete with it.
- **Information constraints:** distinguish capability baselines from the model-generated final result; explain the refinement chain without exposing discarded pages as equal cases.
- **Operation constraints:** one direct link to each runnable example; no save/archive workflow in the user journey.
- **State constraints:** the depth-field result is canonical; prior refinements are research evidence only.
- **Environment constraints:** local Vite runtime at `http://127.0.0.1:8143`; desktop and 390px verification.
- **Primary journey:** understand the two baseline capabilities -> understand how the model-generated fashion result was refined -> open the final full-screen result.
- **Required artifacts:** restored generated bundle, one canonical archived copy, curated overview, refinement record, browser/build evidence.
- **Autonomy authorization:** the user explicitly instructed correction and continuation.
- **User-decision boundary:** a materially different hero result or a new external asset source.

## Quality-gate correction

The R24 gate treated any large text/subject overlap as a failure. Browser review at scroll progress `0.484` shows that the overlap is intentional editorial composition: the transparent garment remains legible, the headline remains readable, and their contact creates the visual tension the brief requested.

Future automatic review must distinguish:

- invalid text-to-text, control-to-text, clipping, and unreadable contrast collisions;
- intentional text-to-transparent-subject overlap that preserves reading order and interaction;
- composition quality, asset integration, narrative continuity, and final visual memory, which cannot be replaced by one overlap ratio.

Mechanical gates may reject broken output, but they must not choose the creative winner without visual-model and human judgment.
