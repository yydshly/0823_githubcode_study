# Wind Kite Lab delivery contract

- Direct run: `direct-wind-kite-lab-r118`.
- Experience architecture: bright immersive Spatial Stage with a persistent Three.js kite scene and semantic DOM controls.
- One shared calibration state drives wind speed, bridle offset, altitude, kite pose, ribbon motion, flow lines, stability and conceptual pull.
- Values are explicitly a visual concept simulation, not weather measurement or safety advice.
- `?fallback=1` preserves the same kite subject, all controls, derived results and save action without WebGL.
- Reduced motion settles the opening immediately and removes continuous flutter while keeping parameter causality.
- Final identity, browser evidence and archive eligibility are recorded only after the bundle is visually inspected and frozen.
